The challenge

You hold the LP relationships. Who holds yours?

Investor relations sits at the centre of the firm and often stands alone. You carry the LP relationships, the fundraising story, and the reporting while partners make the decisions that shape all three.

You are expected to bring clarity to investors and speed to partners, usually in a team of one or two. Yet there are very few places to speak openly with people who understand the work.

A private room for the conversations that cannot happen in a public community.
Who it is for

For the people who make capital relationships work.

You bring real situations to the room and leave with perspective, practical next steps, and peers who understand the stakes.

A practitioner room, selected with care.

Every member carries live LP relationships and contributes from experience, not theory.

  • RoleIR Managers, Directors, and Heads of IR at established VC, PE, or growth firms.
  • FocusFundraising, communications, reporting, or investor engagement with real responsibility for the relationship.
  • ExpectationsPractical peer exchange, candid benchmarks, and no providers selling services in the room.

This room is not for placement agents, fundraising advisers, fund administrators, vendors, investment professionals, or fund founders seeking a fund-building programme.

Questions

Before you join the room.

Can I share LP information?

You share situations, not names. The format is designed for anonymised cases, and everything stays in the room.

Is this for VC or PE?

Both. The cohort is balanced thoughtfully and makes space for the moments where the contexts differ.

How much time does it take?

There is one 90-minute session every two weeks, with optional peer follow-up between sessions.

What if my firm has two IR people?

There is one seat per firm so every member can speak openly about their working environment.
