Our approach

Leadership in private equity is not only about returns.

You already know how to run a deal. This is about the leadership, positioning, economics, and decisions that arrive when there is no playbook and the room is watching.

We create space for reflection, honesty, and the occasional moment of levity. Clarity does not always emerge through analysis alone. It can also grow through connection, creativity, and the freedom to be fully yourself in the room.

The work of becoming a Partner is personal, relational, and deeply human.
Who it is for

A circle for PE investors in transition.

For people building the authority, economics, and investing point of view that the Partner seat demands.

Principal

Taking on Partner-level responsibility and preparing to make the case for the next step.

Investment Director

Developing the commercial judgment, leadership presence, and sponsorship the role requires.

Partner track

Navigating firm dynamics, promotion criteria, and the economics of the seat.

New Partner

In the first two years of the role and shaping an investing signature that is clearly your own.

The work

Five themes for the Partner seat.

A four-month journey built around the situations you are facing now.

01

Reading the Partner room

Understand how decisions and promotions are made, who your sponsors are, and how to show up with authority now.

02

Economics of the seat

Explore carry, vesting, co-invest, GP commitment, and how to negotiate the path in front of you.

03

Investing signature

Clarify your unique edge, your beliefs about capital and risk, and how to articulate them confidently.

04

Difficult conversations

Navigate high-stakes moments with partners, LPs, portfolio leaders, and management teams.

05

Leading the deal team

Work on delegation, feedback, and the leadership expected from you at this stage.

What you receive

Everything in one cohort.

01

Mastermind sessions and coaching

Deep, confidential conversations paired with personalised executive coaching to clarify your voice and next moves.

02

Expert conversations

Selected PE Partners, LPs, and fund advisers who have sat on different sides of the table.

03

Private peer circle

A trusted network of PE investors navigating a similar transition, with no direct competitors.

04

Frameworks and reflection

Useful tools to develop your approach to deals and capital allocation, alongside the presence and self-trust the role asks for.
