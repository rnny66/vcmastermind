## Our approach

### Leadership in private equity is not only about returns. It is personal, relational, and deeply human.

We facilitate the conversations that matter, and we create space for reflection, honesty, and the occasional moment of levity. In our experience clarity does not always emerge through analysis. Sometimes it shows up through connection, creativity, and the freedom to be fully yourself in the room.

## The work

### What we explore together

The work, across four months.

**01\. Reading the Partner room:** how decisions and promotions actually get made, who your sponsors are, and how to show up with authority before the title confirms it.

**02\. The economics of the seat:** carry, vesting, co-invest, and GP commitment, and how to negotiate your path.

**03\. Your investing signature:** what you are uniquely good at, your sector edge, what you believe about capital and risk, and how to articulate it with confidence.

**04\. Difficult conversations, done well:** using brain-based principles to navigate high-stakes moments with partners, LPs, and management teams.

**05\. Leading the deal team:** delegation, feedback, and being the senior one, plus what GPs, LPs, and portfolio management actually need from you at this stage.
