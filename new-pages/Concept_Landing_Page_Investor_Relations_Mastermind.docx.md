# **2\. The problem**

## **You hold the LP relationships. Who holds yours?**

Investor Relations sits at the center of the firm and often stands alone. You carry the LP relationships, the fundraising story and the reporting, while the partners make the decisions that shape all three. You are expected to bring clarity to investors and speed to partners, usually as a team of one or two, and with very few people you can speak openly to. The public communities are big, transactional, and full of people who want to sell you something.

# **3\. This is for you if**

* You are an IR Manager, Director or Head of IR at a VC or PE firm.

* You carry real LP relationships and are involved in fundraising.

* You want to influence your partners without formal authority.

* You want honest benchmarks from peers, not conference slides.

* You are thinking about your next step in the profession.

## **This is not for**

Placement agents, fundraising advisors, fund administrators and other vendors, as well as investment professionals and fund founders looking for their own fund-building program.

# **4\. What you get**

* **A confidential room.** Eight IR professionals, curated for chemistry, with no competitive overlap and no vendors.

* **Real situations, real answers.** Bring live challenges (a tough LP call, a stalled raise, not having the decision power of a partner yeta partner who won't decide) and leave with a next step.

* **Influence and positioningpwositioning.** Practical tools to earn the partners' ear and to be seen as a strategic role, not an executional service function.

* **Sharper LP conversations.** Narrative, hard-news handling, and how to run a fundraising process with calm.

* **Peer benchmarks.** How other firms handle reporting, DDQs, LP communication and tooling.

* **Your next step.** Clarity on your career path in IR and beyond.

Three months, six bi-weekly sessions of 90 minutes, fully remote. \[In-person gathering optional, to discuss.\]

# **6\. Who is in the room**

Each cohort has a maximum of eight members, selected through an application and a short conversation. We balance venture capital and private equity, career stage and team size, and we make sure that no two people from the same firm, or competing for the same LP pool, sit in the same room. Everyone signs a confidentiality agreement and works under Chatham House rules.

# **10\. FAQ**

* **Can I really share LP information?** You share situations, not names. The format works with anonymised cases, and everything stays in the room.

* **Is this for VC or PE?** Both. We balance the room and cover the differences in dedicated moments.

* **How much time does it take?** 90 minutes every two weeks, plus optional peer follow-up between sessions.

* **What if my firm has two IR people?** Only one seat per firm to protect openness.

* **What if I am new in the role?** Welcome, as long as you already carry LP contact and are involved in fundraising.
