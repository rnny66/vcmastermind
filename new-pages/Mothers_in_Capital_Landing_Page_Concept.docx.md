## **Our Approach**

We believe this juggle is common and not something to manage on your own behind closed doors. It's not a personal failing to manage quietly, it's a structural reality to navigate with the right people in a safe space. Alongside rigorous investment conversation, we create space for the parts of the job no one puts in the LP deck: the guilt, the logistics, the identity shift, and the moments of real joy in doing both.
