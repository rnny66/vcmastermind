Our Approach

We believe stewardship is not only strategic. It is personal, relational, and expressive. Alongside rigorous conversation, we create space for reflection, honesty, and moments of levity, because clarity often emerges not just through analysis, but through connection, creativity, and being fully yourself.

Who This Is For

A circle for women in transition

From beneficiary to active steward, wherever you are on that path.

  • Stepping into or preparing to inherit, manage, or influence significant wealth
  • Navigating complex family dynamics, expectations, or governance structures
  • Seeking to define their own voice and philosophy around money and investing
  • Curious about how to grow and deploy capital in aligned and meaningful ways
  • Ready to move from beneficiary to active steward

Anieke Lamers

Facilitator · Founder, The VC Coach

A VC with 15+ years of venture experience and the founder of The VC Coach. She has facilitated multiple peer mastermind groups, combining deep investment expertise with a human-centered coaching approach: a space where family fund investors can tackle strategic, operational, and personal challenges openly, and leave with practical insight and a trusted network.

Elizabeth Scutchfield

Facilitator · Strategy & Systems

Strategic advisor and systems thinker with 15+ years at the intersection of leadership, legacy, and global systems change, having supported visionary leaders across five continents. She has convened multiple women’s peer circles, and, informed by her own journey of stepping into responsibility around family resources, creates spaces where women move from hesitation into grounded authorship over how they lead, invest, and contribute.

Liliana Negrila

Guest Speaker · Former LP & Coach

Coach and facilitator working with fund managers and investors on the human dynamics of the role, with advisory work for VC funds on fundraising strategy and LP engagement. Liliana brings a deep understanding of the interpersonal and organisational challenges that define the experience.
