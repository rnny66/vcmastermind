Our approach

Ambition and motherhood do not need to cancel each other out.

This is not about choosing between the fund and the family. It is about building the judgment, the language, and the peer group to hold both, without pretending one does not affect the other.

Careers in venture capital and private equity are demanding by definition. Long hours and travel are a given, and things like carry and the promotion path depend largely on the fund you work at. With no standard to measure against, it is harder to plan for the years when you are both an investor and a mother. It can feel overwhelming, especially early on, and it can feel unsafe to talk about it at all.

This is a room for that conversation, with women in the same situation who want honest discussion and practical answers. We cover the guilt, the logistics and the identity shift too. You leave knowing what you can change, what you can accept, and what to ask for.

A private, curated circle for mothers in VC and PE ready to lead with clarity, on their own terms, in the deal room and at home.
Who it is for

A circle for mothers navigating both.

Wherever you are between maternity leave and the Partner track.

You are:

  • Investing in venture capital or private equity, at any seniority from Associate to Partner.
  • Returning from, approaching, or several years past parental leave, and renegotiating what ambition looks like now.
  • Navigating the travel-heavy parts of the job (roadshows, LP meetings, portfolio visits) alongside childcare reality.
  • Tired of being the only mother in the room, or the only investor in your mothers’ group (if you even have time for that).
  • Ready to build a career plan that is honest about this season of life, without lowering the ambition.
  • Not interested in a whiney moms club. This is for ambitious women looking for constructive conversations to navigate this in the best way possible.
What we explore

The work, together.

Each theme surfaces through the real decisions you bring to the room.

Returning and remaining visible

Ambition and visibility

Renegotiate ambition and visibility after leave without starting over. Reclaim your pipeline, your deal credit and your place in the partner conversation.

Protecting your seat

Handle travel, board seats, and deal flow on terms that work for your life and career.

Redesigning the terms of seniority

Asking for flexibility

Name and ask for the flexibility you need without it reading as reduced commitment, and keep evening and travel boundaries without apologising.

Economics and interruption

Talk plainly about carry, compensation, and the financial reality of a career interruption.

Integrating leadership and motherhood

Your leadership model

Build your own model of what ‘senior investor and present mother’ looks like, including what to outsource, what to protect and what to let go.

Identity and guilt

Make space for the identity shift, the guilt, and the parts of this no LP deck ever mentions.
