Our approach

Ambition and motherhood do not need to cancel each other out.

This is not about choosing between the fund and the family. It is about building the judgment, language, and peer group to hold both without pretending one does not affect the other.

Alongside rigorous investment conversation, we make space for the parts of the job no one puts in the LP deck: the logistics, the identity shift, the moments of guilt, and the real joy of doing both.

A private, ambitious room for the deal room and everything around it.
Who it is for

A circle for mothers navigating both.

Wherever you are between maternity leave and the Partner track, this is a space for a career plan that is honest about the season you are in.

You are welcome in the room if you are:

  • Investing in venture capital or private equity, from Associate to Partner.
  • Returning from leave, approaching it, or rethinking what ambition looks like years after it.
  • Navigating travel, boards, deal flow, and childcare without lowering your ambition.
  • Looking for a confidential peer group that understands both the professional and personal stakes.
