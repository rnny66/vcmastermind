Are you eligible?

Three conditions.

01

Dutch SME with staff

Registered with the KVK, with at least 1 employee on payroll.

02

SME status incl. partners

Affiliated and partner companies count toward determining your SME status.

03

Sufficient so-called "de-minimis headroom"

You still have enough room under the European "de minimis" ceiling for state aid.

Less suited: sole traders without staff, individuals seeking private coaching, companies wanting to have only the director coached, and enterprises that don't qualify as an SME.
